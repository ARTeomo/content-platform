## Final Verdict

# **CONDITIONAL PASS**

**Justification (Observation → Evidence → Impact → Verdict):**

1. **The baseline findings' remediation is verified at code level** — not merely documented: F1/F2/F3/F4/F7 closed with actual runtime wiring (file-level evidence above); claim idempotency and the fail-closed rate limiter exist in the code, not just in HANDOFF.
2. **No stability, data-consistency, or architectural blocker exists** — the outbox/claim/scheduler/reconciler backbone is consistent and idempotent; since the Phase 19e real E2E (a real publication), the backbone has not changed structurally.
3. **The CONDITIONAL condition lies not in the repository but in the v1.3 planning documents:** the plan and the contract revision proceed from three facts that collide with the actual state (journal end `0012` vs. actual `0014`; AAD `META:${destination_id}` vs. actual `META:WEBHOOK_VERIFY_TOKEN:…`; F5/F6 "missing" vs. actual "partially done, with deviating semantics"). Without these three corrections (N1, N2, N3+N4), starting v1.3 would build **on facts recorded incorrectly in the plan** — per the audit work order §23, this is MUST FIX **before development starts**, as a revision of the plan.
4. **Not blocking, but weakening the evidence chain:** due to the absence of test execution and the missing gate tests (N5), the runtime verification of the "208 passing" claim and the gate behavior remains open — the first phases of v1.3 close this.

**Answer to the final question:** starting from today's repository state, v1.3 development **can begin safely, technically and architecturally**, without touching the v1.2/baseline foundation layer — **conditional on** the v1.3 plan and the `DATABASE_SCHEMA_CONTRACT.md` v1.3 revision receiving the three corrections (migration numbering from `0015`; real AAD binding; F5/F6 re-scope). This constitutes **CONDITIONAL PASS**: the condition is fulfillable at the document level, as the first step of development.
